Our services

Specialists in bees, wasps, and hornets

The Bee Man of Wisconsin specializes in the control of bees, wasps and hornets. Most full service pest control companies have very little experience with bees, wasps and hornets — we have the knowledge, chemicals and specialized tools that are not available to the general public.

Bee & Wasp Abatement

A preventive service that continues to control the nesting of stinging insects for the entire season. Best performed in spring to early summer — your entire property is then guaranteed against nesting until December 31st.

One Spot Treatment

We exterminate individual nests — visible or hidden in a wall void — and guarantee that we will kill all treated nests. The nest will usually die within a few hours.

Boxelder Bug & Asian Lady Beetle Abatement

One fall service for both pests: a crack and crevice treatment, performed in fall when boxelder bugs and Asian lady beetles seek a place to overwinter. It prevents more than ninety percent of these pests from invading your home.

Bald-Faced Hornet Nest Removal

Starting in July and continuing through the fall, when conditions are appropriate, we remove bald-faced hornet nests during night hours, when all of the hornets are in the nest.

About us

It started with a letter to a radio show in 1980

The Bee Man of Wisconsin, founded by Curt Jepson, started as a conversation with his brother, Chris, in 1979. In 1980, Curt wrote a letter to WTMJ radio host Gordon Hinkley detailing the free service he was offering — removing bald-faced hornet nests. Mr. Hinkley read Curt’s letter on the air along with his phone number. The phone started ringing and has never stopped!

His callers would initially ask for “the name and number of that guy that takes down those nests.” Eventually, they just started asking for the “Bee Man.” The name stuck!
